ABOUT HERITAGE

Stories Written in Stone

Heritage represents the history, culture, architecture and traditions passed from one generation to another.

India has a rich heritage that includes ancient temples, royal forts, beautiful palaces and historic monuments.

Exploring heritage places helps us understand the history, architecture and culture of different parts of India.
